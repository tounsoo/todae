Arrows at a scroller's cut-off edges. `ScrollArea.Root` takes `arrows` to show an arrow at each edge where its content is cut off, and resting a mouse or pen on one scrolls that way, faster the longer it rests (one even speed under reduced motion). Popover (`arrows`, on by default), Menu and its submenus, and the DateTimePicker time list show them when they scroll. New tokens `scroll.arrow.size`, `scroll.arrow.fg` and `scroll.arrow.bg`.
