# Product tour

A recipe, not a package namespace. A tour walks the person through a few controls one at a time: one [Coachmark](/coachmark) points at each in turn, and your product keeps the steps, their order and whether the tour has been seen. There is no `Tour.*` export.

## Steps

Keep the steps in an array, each with its title, its text and a ref to its target, and the current step in state. Render one `Coachmark.Root` for the whole tour and hand it the current step's `targetRef` and text, so moving on keeps the popup open and only moves it and the hole to the next target. Closing the coachmark, by Escape or Done, ends the tour.

## Focus on each step

Pass the Next button as `initialFocusRef`, so focus lands there as the tour starts. Next is the same element on every step, Done included, so focus stays on it as the tour moves on. Back is only there after the first step: as it goes back to the first, it moves focus to Next before it unmounts. If the person presses the target itself and that moves the tour on, the old target turns inert, and the coachmark moves focus into the popup.

Since focus stays put, a screen reader doesn't read the new step by itself. Give `Coachmark.Description` `aria-live="polite"`, so its new text is read as the step changes.

## Remembering the tour

Store that the tour was seen where your product stores other settings, such as the person's account, and start it again only when they ask, such as from a help menu. A tour that opens by itself on every visit makes the page inert each time.

## Accessible composition

**Todae handles:** Everything [Coachmark](/coachmark) does for one step: the named dialog, the inert page around the target, the Tab cycle, Escape, and focus back where it was when the tour ends. When `targetRef` changes, the anchor, the hole and the inert page move to the new target.

**You supply:** The steps and their order. Next, Back and Done buttons, and a step count in words, such as "2 of 3". The live region on the description. Where the tour's state is stored.

**Watch for:** A step whose target isn't on the page, such as one in a closed panel: open the panel first, or leave the step out. Steps that only repeat each control's name.

## When to use

**When:** A few new or hard-to-find controls on one page, shown once, or again when the person asks.

**When not:** One control → [Coachmark](/coachmark) alone. A setup the person has to finish, with answers → [Wizard](/recipes/wizard). Help that should stay on the page → text beside the control or a [Tooltip](/tooltip).
