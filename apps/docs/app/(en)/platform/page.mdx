# Native platform

Chrome is Todae's target, along with the other browsers on Chromium, such as Edge. Safari and Firefox are supported too, on one condition: nothing may break there. Every component works in them, apart from the known Firefox issues below, but some polish is Chrome-only, and the last section of this page lists it.

Todae builds on the browser instead of reimplementing it. It uses the features below with no JavaScript fallbacks, so a browser that has them is a browser Todae supports. This page lists those features. The version floor is the [Browser support](https://github.com/tounsoo/todae/blob/main/README.md#browser-support) table in the repository README, checked on 2026-10-16.

CSS anchor positioning sets the floor in every browser. In Chrome and Edge the floor is `position-try-fallbacks`, which arrived in 128. On iOS and iPadOS, every browser uses Safari's engine, so Chrome and Edge there follow the Safari row, and the Safari lines below. The versions are not pinned in code. Component browser tests run in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, in builds newer than these floors, so the floors themselves are not tested.

A browser test that fails in Chromium or WebKit fails CI. Firefox has known issues, tracked in [issue 367](https://github.com/tounsoo/todae/issues/367). The Firefox tests those issues break still run in CI, but their failures show as warnings instead of failing it, and any other Firefox failure fails it as before. Firefox goes back to failing CI on every failure before 1.0 ships.

- **[CSS anchor positioning](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/CSS_anchor_positioning)**: placing popovers and tooltips next to their trigger, and flipping them at the viewport edge with `position-try-fallbacks`, placing `Field.Textarea`'s highlights over the textarea, and placing `Coachmark.Root`'s popup and the hole in its dim page over its target. Without it: the surface is not placed next to its trigger and sits near the top-left corner of the viewport, the highlights are not drawn, and a coachmark dims the whole page, its target too, with no hole.
- **[Popover API](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Popover_API)**: showing `Popover.Root`, `Tooltip.Root`, `Menu`'s lists, `Coachmark.Root` and the toasts of `Toast.Region` in the top layer. Todae needs it.
- **[`<dialog>`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/HTML/Element/dialog) and `showModal()`**: `Dialog.Root`, `Drawer.Root` and `BottomSheet.Root`, for the top layer, `::backdrop`, an inert page behind, Escape, and `<form method="dialog">`. Todae needs it. The scrim reads Todae's custom properties from `::backdrop`, which inherits them from its dialog in current browsers; an older engine that does not shows no scrim color.
- **[`closedby`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/HTML/Element/dialog#closedby)**: `Dialog`, `Drawer` and `BottomSheet` with `dismissOnEscape={false}`, so the browser ignores Escape and the back gesture, except while a layer inside can take them: an open popover or menu, or a `Drilldown` with a view to go back to. A tooltip takes Escape only, so it doesn't change `closedby`. Without it: Escape still asks to close, and Todae holds the surface open.
- **[`inert`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/HTML/Global_attributes/inert)**: hiding the chip measuring copy, a `Drilldown` view while it slides out, a popover, menu, tooltip or coachmark while it fades out, and the page around `Coachmark.Root`'s target. Todae needs it.
- **[`getAnimations()`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Element/getAnimations)**: keeping a `Drilldown` view that slides out on screen until its own transitions finish. Todae needs it. `Coachmark.Root` also uses it to wait out its slide to a new target, and a tooltip to wait until its trigger stops moving or scaling. Without it: those two don't wait.
- **[`checkVisibility()`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Element/checkVisibility)**: a `Drilldown` that isn't rendered, such as one in a closed popover or a hidden tab panel, passes Escape and close requests on. Todae needs it. Todae also uses it to tell hidden elements from shown ones when it moves focus, when `ScrollArea` and `Conversation` decide whether their scroller takes Tab, and when `OptionList` scrolls its active option into view. Without it: `OptionList` treats every list as shown, and `ScrollArea`, `Conversation` and focus moves see only an inline `display: none` and the `hidden` attribute.
- **[`<details name>`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/HTML/Element/details#name)**: exclusive accordions. Without it: several disclosures in a group can be open at once.
- **[`::details-content`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/::details-content)**: fading a disclosure's content in. Without it: the content appears at once.
- **[`field-sizing: content`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/field-sizing)**: `Field.Textarea` with `autoGrow`. Without it: the textarea keeps its rows and scrolls.
- **[`document.execCommand('insertText')`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Document/execCommand)**: a `useMentions` pick and a `Field.OneTimeCode` paste, so each is one step on the input's undo stack. Without it: the hook sets the text itself, and undo may not bring back the typed word; `Field.OneTimeCode` sets the value itself and fires an `input` event, and undo may not bring back the old code.
- **[`autocomplete="one-time-code"`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/HTML/Attributes/autocomplete#one-time-code)**: `Field.OneTimeCode`, so a phone can offer the code from a text message. Without it: the person types or pastes the code.
- **[`@scope`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/@scope)**: `Prose.Root` styles markdown's elements up to the first element of another kind. Without it: headings, lists and quotes inside the prose take the browser's own styles.
- **[`:dir()`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/:dir)**: `Splitter.Handle` asks whether its element is right-to-left (`matches(':dir(rtl)')`) to point the arrow keys and the drag the way the handle moves. Todae needs it.
- **[`:has()`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/:has)**: `Field` and `Chip` focus rings and adornment layout, the `Alert` columns, the strip behind a `Calendar` range, and stopping page scroll while a modal `<dialog>` is open (with `:modal`). Todae needs it.
- **[`text-wrap`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/text-wrap)**: `balance` on the `EmptyState.Title` and `pretty` on `EmptyState.Description`, to even out line lengths and avoid a lone last word. Without it: lines break raggedly, which is purely cosmetic.
- **[`-webkit-line-clamp`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/line-clamp)**: clamping `Text.Root` to its `lines`, together with `display: -webkit-box`. Todae needs it.
- **[`scrollbar-gutter`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/scrollbar-gutter)**: keeping the page's scrollbar space while a modal `<dialog>` stops page scroll. Without it: the page shifts by the scrollbar's width when a modal opens.
- **[Cascade layers](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/@layer)**: ordering Todae's reset, tokens, components and a company's theme. Todae needs it.
- **[Relative color syntax](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/color_value/oklch)**: translucent colors made from a token, such as `color.bg.selected`. Todae needs it.
- **[`@starting-style`](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/@starting-style)**: `Dialog` entry transitions, and a `Drilldown` view sliding in. Without it: the dialog and the view appear without a transition.
- **[Temporal](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Temporal)**: the values of `Calendar`, `Field.DateInput`, `Field.DateRangeInput`, `Field.TimeInput` and `Field.DateTimeInput`, and of `formatDate`, `parseDate`, `formatDateRange`, `parseDateRange`, `formatTime`, `parseTime`, `formatDateTime`, `parseDateTime`, `formatRelativeTime` and `timeOptions`. Without it, unless the app loads a Temporal polyfill: `Calendar.Root` throws when it renders, `timeOptions` and `formatDateRange` throw, `formatRelativeTime` throws when the two times are an hour or more apart, and `parseDate`, `parseDateRange`, `parseTime` and `parseDateTime` can throw, and so can a typed input when it reads its text.
- **[Async Clipboard API](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Clipboard/writeText)**: `useClipboard`'s copy. Without it, or on a page that is not a secure context: the copy fails and `status` is `'failed'`.
- **[Intersection Observer](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Intersection_Observer_API)**, with a `Document` as `root`: `Contents.Root` follows the sections of the page with it. Todae needs it.
- **[Constraint Validation API](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/HTML/Constraint_validation)**: `Form.Root` and its validation scopes read each control's `validity`, `willValidate` and `validationMessage`, check the form with `checkValidity()`, read `SubmitEvent.submitter` for `formNoValidate`, and set `customValidity` with `setCustomValidity()`. `requestSubmit()` goes through the same check. Todae needs it.
- **[`dvh` units](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/length#dynamic)**: `Dialog` and `BottomSheet` heights that follow mobile browser chrome. Todae needs it.

Behavior that matters most is tested in real browsers (Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, through Playwright): Escape order across stacked overlays, modal focus and restore, flip at the viewport edge, chip overflow measurement and bottom-sheet drag. Every docs example is also checked with axe in Chromium.

## What Safari and Firefox do differently

Each line says what Chrome does and what Safari and Firefox do instead. Motion only has to work in Chrome, and under reduced motion every browser skips the slides and height changes below. Some of these need a newer Chrome than the floor above, up to Chrome 140, and an older Chrome does what Safari and Firefox do.

- **[Disclosure](/disclosure) and [Nav](/nav) branches**: grow and shrink to their height as they open and close. Both change the height at once: Safari fades the content in and out, and Firefox fades it in and hides it at once as it closes.
- **[Toast](/toast)**: in a list, gives back its height as it leaves, so the toasts behind it slide into place. Safari and Firefox move them once it is gone.
- **[Conversation](/conversation)'s newest message**: grows into place. Safari and Firefox fade it in at full size.
- **`Conversation.ScrollButton`**: fades out. Firefox hides it at once.
- **[Tabs](/tabs)**: the indicator slides to the new tab. Safari and Firefox move it at once.
- **[Coachmark](/coachmark)**: the hole and popup slide to a tour's next target. Safari and Firefox move them at once.
- **[Progress](/progress) and Meter**: the fill glides to a new value. Firefox jumps.
- **[`Field.Select`](/field/select)**: draws its button and list from Todae's tokens, as Safari 27 does too. Firefox and Safari 26 show the browser's own list, with the same value and keys, and keep the browser's arrow instead of the `chevron` glyph.
- **`Field.Textarea` with `autoGrow`**: grows with its text. Firefox before 152 and Safari before 26.2 keep its rows and scroll inside it.
- **`Field.Select` in a field**: a press beside the select, such as on an `Icon`, opens its list. Safari only focuses the select.
- **[Contents](/contents)**: marks the current link from CSS as well, before its script runs. Safari and Firefox mark it once the script runs.
- **[EmptyState](/empty-state)'s description**: avoids a lone last word, as Safari does too. Firefox breaks its lines raggedly.
