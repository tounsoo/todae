# Fieldset

A native `<fieldset>` that groups related controls under one `<legend>`, such as a set of checkboxes. A screen reader reads the legend as the group's name when focus enters it.

```tsx
<Fieldset.Root invalid={none} aria-describedby={none ? errorId : undefined}>
	<Fieldset.Legend>Email topics</Fieldset.Legend>
	<Checkbox>Product news</Checkbox>
	<Checkbox>Security notices</Checkbox>
	{none ? <ErrorMessage id={errorId}>Pick at least one topic.</ErrorMessage> : null}
</Fieldset.Root>
```

<Example block>
  <FieldsetCheckboxes />
</Example>

`invalid` marks every Todae control inside invalid: a Checkbox, Switch, RadioGroup or Field gets `aria-invalid` and its invalid style. A fieldset has no `aria-invalid` of its own, so say why with a [Description](/description) or [ErrorMessage](/errormessage) and point the root's `aria-describedby` at it.

`disabled` is the native attribute: the browser disables every form control inside, and Todae's controls show their disabled style. A fieldset inside a disabled one is disabled too. Inside an invalid fieldset, a control or nested fieldset is invalid unless it sets `invalid={false}`, for a field the person got right. Controls inside the first `Fieldset.Legend` stay enabled, as the browser keeps them.

The parts stack with `fieldset.gap`. For controls side by side, put them in a `Stack direction="row"`.

## Accessible composition

**Todae handles:** `Fieldset.Root` is a native `<fieldset>`, so it has the `group` role and its first `Fieldset.Legend` is the group's name. `disabled` is the native attribute: every form control inside is disabled and leaves the tab order, except those in that first legend. `invalid` sets `aria-invalid` on each Todae control inside: Checkbox, Switch, `RadioGroup.Root` and its radios, and the control of a `Field.Root`. Controls in that first legend are left out of `invalid` too. Nested fieldsets inherit both.

**You supply:** A `Fieldset.Legend` as the first child, with text that names the question. The fieldset has no `aria-invalid` of its own, so point `aria-describedby` on the root at an [ErrorMessage](/errormessage) or [Description](/description) that says why. `invalid={false}` on a control the person got right.

**Watch for:** A Legend wrapped in another element, or after another legend, does not name the group, and the controls in it follow the fieldset's `disabled`. `disabled` is not read-only: disabled controls cannot be focused, so keyboard users cannot reach their values. A styled `div` in place of the fieldset drops the group and its name.

## When to use

**When:** Several controls answer one question: a checkbox group, a set of switches, or the fields of an address. Name the group with Fieldset.Legend.

**When not:** One field → Label with Field.Root. A single choice among options → RadioGroup, which is its own group. Sections of a long form with their own headings → headings.
