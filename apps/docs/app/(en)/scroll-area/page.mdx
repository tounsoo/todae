# ScrollArea

`ScrollArea.Root` is a `div` that scrolls and keeps the focus rings of what it holds whole. A scroll container clips everything past its padding box, so a ring around a control at its edge is cut. `ScrollArea.Root` puts its children in a content box padded by a ring's width and offset, inside any padding you give the area, so the room is always there and nothing reaches past the area's edge. When focus lands on a control whose ring would still be cut, it scrolls the rest of the ring into view.

</Example>

```tsx
<nav aria-label="Guide sections">
  <ScrollArea.Root style={{ blockSize: '9rem' }}>
    <ul>{/* links */}</ul>
  </ScrollArea.Root>
</nav>
```

`axis` picks the direction it scrolls in, `block` by default, and clips the other. Give it a size and padding the way you would any `div`. Lay its children out with an element inside it, since they sit in its content box. It has no server entry, so a Server Component renders it inside a client component.

## A Tab stop for content with no controls

When its content overflows and nothing inside can take focus, a keyboard could not reach the rest. So it then takes a Tab stop with the `region` role, and the arrow keys scroll it. Name it with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`; it warns in development when it takes a stop without a name. Its own ring is drawn inside its edge from `scroll.ring.focus`.

</Example>

```tsx
<ScrollArea.Root aria-label="Release notes" style={{ blockSize: '7rem' }}>
  <Text.Root>…</Text.Root>
</ScrollArea.Root>
```

`focusable` sets the stop either way: `true` keeps one while it overflows even with controls inside, and `false` never takes one, for a list of links that focus already scrolls through.

## Accessible composition

**Todae handles:** Room for focus rings at every edge, and scrolling so focus brings a whole ring into view. A Tab stop with the `region` role while the content overflows and holds nothing focusable, kept while focus is on it so a resize never drops focus, and removed once the content fits. Its own focus ring, inside its edge.

**You supply:** A name with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` whenever it can take a Tab stop. A height or width that makes it scroll.

**Watch for:** Layout styles such as `display: grid` or `gap` on `ScrollArea.Root`: its only child is its content box, so they never reach your children. Put them on an element inside it. A row of items that should keep their width when it scrolls sideways: as in any scrolling `div`, flex and grid items shrink to their narrowest before the row scrolls. Give them `flex: none` or a fixed track size.

## When to use

**When:** A region that scrolls on its own, such as a sidebar, a list in a panel or a row of cards that scrolls sideways.

**When not:** The page itself scrolls → let it, with no scroll area. Content that should fold rows away rather than scroll → [ChipList](/chip-list) or `maxRows` on [Field](/field). A sheet's body → [BottomSheet](/bottomsheet)'s `BottomSheet.Body`, which already scrolls and leaves the same room.
