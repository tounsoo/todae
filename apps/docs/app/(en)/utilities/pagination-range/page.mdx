# paginationRange

`paginationRange({ page, count, siblings, boundaries })` returns the pages to show for `page` out of `count`: page numbers, with `"start-ellipsis"` and `"end-ellipsis"` for the gaps. It shows the current page, `siblings` pages on each side of it and `boundaries` pages at each end; both default to 1. [Pagination](/pagination) renders the list.

## A steady length

While there are more pages than slots, the list keeps one length, so the number of controls doesn't change as the page does and nothing jumps under the pointer. With the defaults, that is seven items: page 1 of 20 gives `1, 2, 3, 4, 5, end-ellipsis, 20` and page 10 gives `1, start-ellipsis, 9, 10, 11, end-ellipsis, 20`. A gap always stands for two or more pages, so where an ellipsis would hide one page, that page is shown instead. A `count` below 1 gives an empty list, and a `page` outside 1 to `count` is moved to the nearest end.

## Accessible composition

**Todae handles:** Nothing on screen: it returns the list. [Pagination](/pagination) gives it the landmark, the current page and the ellipses.

**You supply:** A link or button for each page number, with `aria-current="page"` on the current one, as [Pagination](/pagination) shows.

**Watch for:** Keying items by their index: key a page by its number and an ellipsis by its name, since both ellipses can show at once.

## When to use

**When:** The page numbers for [Pagination](/pagination), or for paging controls of your own, such as under a [Table](/table).

**When not:** A list people scroll through without pages → load more as they reach the end.
