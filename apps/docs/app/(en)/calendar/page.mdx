# Calendar

`Calendar.Root` holds one selected date as a `Temporal.PlainDate`, or with `mode="range"` a range of dates. `Calendar.Header` shows the month and year between buttons to the previous and next month, and `Calendar.Grid` shows that month's days, one week to a row. Pressing the month and year swaps the days for a page of twelve years, and the buttons beside it go to the previous and next page. Picking a year shows its month again, keeping the month and day where it can. The root takes `value` with `onValueChange`, or `defaultValue`, and calls `onValueChange` with the date a person picks.

```tsx
<Calendar.Root aria-label="Start date" value={date} onValueChange={setDate}>
  <Calendar.Header />
  <Calendar.Grid />
</Calendar.Root>
```

For a date in a form, use [Field.DatePicker](/field/date-picker): a button in a field that opens this calendar in a popover.

## Range and locale

`min` and `max` bound the dates that can be picked, the month buttons stop at their months, and years outside them are disabled. `isDateDisabled` rules out other dates, such as weekends or holidays. Days outside `min` and `max` are disabled buttons. A date `isDateDisabled` rules out can still take focus, so a screen reader can read it, but a press or Enter does not pick it.

With no value, the grid starts on `defaultMonth`, else on today's month, kept within `min` and `max`. Month and weekday names come from `locale`, else the browser's own locale. The first column is `firstDayOfWeek`, from 1 (Monday) to 7 (Sunday); without it, the calendar uses the locale's first day where the browser reports one through `Intl.Locale`, else Sunday.

A server render does not know today, so with no `value` or `defaultMonth` the grid has no weeks until the page hydrates, and today is marked only then. When the calendar renders on a server, also pass `locale` and `firstDayOfWeek`, so the server and the browser draw the same names and columns.

## Ranges

With `mode="range"`, `Calendar.Root` holds a `DateRange`, `{ start, end }` with both days included, and `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` take one. The first pick starts a range and the second ends it, in either order, and `onValueChange` gets the range once the second date is picked; picking the same day twice makes a one-day range. Until then the grid shows the range from the first pick to the hovered day, or else the focused day, and Escape drops the first pick. A range holds only days that can be picked, so after the first pick, a day past one that `isDateDisabled`, `min` or `max` rules out can't end it. To find that day, `isDateDisabled` is called for each day from the first pick to the shown month, within `min` and `max`, until it rules one out. This scan reuses its answers while `isDateDisabled`, the first pick, `min` and `max` stay the same, so a function made anew on each render scans again, and one that reads changing data, such as booked days, should be made anew when that data changes. Apart from the scan, each day the grid draws is checked on every render. A `value` set from outside moves the grid to its start and drops a first pick. On the page of years, every year the range touches is selected, or while a range waits for its end, only the first pick's year.

```tsx
<Calendar.Root aria-label="Stay" mode="range" value={range} onValueChange={setRange}>
  <Calendar.Header />
  <Calendar.Grid />
</Calendar.Root>
```

## Temporal

Todae ships no Temporal polyfill. `Calendar.Root` calls `Temporal` when it renders, so where the browser has no Temporal, the app loads a polyfill (see [Native platform](/platform)).

## Accessible composition

**Todae handles:** `Calendar.Root` is a `role="group"`. `Calendar.Grid` is a `role="grid"` table, labelled by the header's month while a `Calendar.Header` is rendered, with a column header for each weekday whose `abbr` is the full name. It is one Tab stop: arrow keys move by day and week, Home and End to the ends of the week, Page Up and Page Down by month, and with Shift by year. Left and Right swap in a right-to-left page. Each day is a button named with the full date, the selected one's cell has `aria-selected`, and today has `aria-current="date"`. Dates `isDateDisabled` rules out have `aria-disabled`, and days outside `min` and `max` are disabled. The month and year is a button with `aria-expanded`, true while the years show, inside a live region, so a screen reader hears it change. The page of years is a `role="grid"` with the same single Tab stop: arrow keys move by year and row, Home and End to the ends of the row, and Page Up and Page Down by page. The buttons beside the heading are named from the `previousMonth` and `nextMonth` strings in [StringsProvider](/utilities/strings-provider), or `previousYears` and `nextYears` while the years show, and they and the heading draw the `chevron` glyph from [GlyphsProvider](/utilities/glyphs-provider). `disabled`, or a disabled [Fieldset](/fieldset), disables every button. In range mode, the grid and the page of years have `aria-multiselectable`, and every day or year the range touches has `aria-selected`, or while a range waits for its end, only the first pick's. After the first pick, the `pickEndDate` string is announced through a live region, and the days that can't end the range have `aria-disabled` and still take focus. Escape then drops the first pick and stops there, so a popover around the calendar stays open.

**You supply:** A name for the group, with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, and one for the grid when there is no `Calendar.Header`. `locale` when the page's language is not the browser's.

**Watch for:** Ruling dates out with `isDateDisabled` but not saying why. Explain the rule nearby, such as "Weekdays only".

## When to use

**When:** Pick one date from a calendar shown on the page, such as on a booking screen.

**When not:** A date in a form field → [Field.DatePicker](/field/date-picker). A range of dates in a form field → [Field.DateRangePicker](/field/date-range-picker). A date with a time → [Field.DateTimePicker](/field/date-time-picker).
