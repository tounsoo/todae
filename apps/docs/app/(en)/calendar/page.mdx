# Calendar

`Calendar.Root` holds one selected date as a `Temporal.PlainDate`, or with `mode="range"` a range of dates. `Calendar.Header` shows the month and year between buttons to the previous and next month, and `Calendar.Grid` shows that month's days, one week to a row. Pressing the month and year swaps the days for a page of twelve years, and the buttons beside it go to the previous and next page. Picking a year shows its month again, keeping the month and day where it can. The root takes `value` with `onValueChange`, or `defaultValue`, and calls `onValueChange` with the date a person picks.

For a date in a form, use a [Field.DateInput](/field/date-input) with a [Field.Picker](/field/picker) beside it that opens this calendar in a popover.

## Range and locale

`min` and `max` bound the dates that can be picked, the month buttons stop at their months, and years outside them are disabled. `isDateDisabled` rules out other dates, such as weekends or holidays. Days outside `min` and `max` are disabled buttons. A date `isDateDisabled` rules out can still take focus, so a screen reader can read it, but a press or Enter does not pick it.

With no value, the grid starts on `defaultMonth`, else on today's month, kept within `min` and `max`. Month and weekday names come from `locale`, else the browser's own locale. The first column is `firstDayOfWeek`, from 1 (Monday) to 7 (Sunday); without it, the calendar uses the locale's first day where the browser reports one through `Intl.Locale`, else Sunday.

A server render does not know today, so with no `value` or `defaultMonth` the grid has no weeks until the page hydrates, and today is marked only then. When the calendar renders on a server, also pass `locale` and `firstDayOfWeek`, so the server and the browser draw the same names and columns.

## Ranges

With `mode="range"`, `Calendar.Root` holds a `DateRange`, `{ start, end }` with both days included, and `value`, `defaultValue` and `onValueChange` take one. The first pick starts a range and the second ends it, in either order, and `onValueChange` gets the range once the second date is picked; picking the same day twice makes a one-day range. Until then the grid shows the range from the first pick to the hovered day, or else the focused day, when that day can end the range, and otherwise only the first pick. Escape drops the first pick, as does a modal's close request such as the Android back gesture, and so does a change to `min`, `max` or `isDateDisabled` that rules it out. A range holds only days that can be picked, so after the first pick, a day past one that `isDateDisabled`, `min` or `max` rules out can't end it. To find that day, `isDateDisabled` is called for each day from the first pick to the shown month, within `min` and `max`, until it rules one out. A render checks at most about two years of those days, and the rest are checked in short slices between frames; until the scan reaches a day, that day can't end the range. This scan reuses its answers while `isDateDisabled`, the first pick, `min` and `max` stay the same, so a function made anew on each render scans again, and a scan that hasn't finished also starts over when any of them changes, so a far range needs a function that stays the same; in development, `Calendar.Root` logs an error when new functions keep starting an unfinished scan over. One that reads changing data, such as booked days, should be made anew when that data changes, and the scan then starts again from the first pick. Apart from the scan, each day the grid draws is checked on every render. A new `value` equal to one of the last eight picks reported that no value has answered yet is taken as the parent's answer to that pick, however late it comes, so it neither moves the grid nor drops a range started since; that pick and the older ones then count as answered, so quick picks can be answered in turn. Any other new `value` drops a first pick and moves the grid to its start, unless the focused day is one of its ends. So a parent that turns a pick down and later sets that same value itself, with no other value between, doesn't move the grid to it. On the page of years, every year the range touches is selected, or while a range waits for its end, only the first pick's year.

## Temporal

Todae ships no Temporal polyfill. `Calendar.Root` calls `Temporal` when it renders, so where the browser has no Temporal, the app loads a polyfill (see [Native platform](/platform)).

## Day content

`renderDay` on `Calendar.Grid` draws extra content inside each day button the grid draws. The grid calls it with that day's `Temporal.PlainDate` and with `outside`, `today`, `selected` and `disabled`. `outside` is a day in the leading or trailing week, and those days render only when `showOutsideDays` is set. `disabled` means the day cannot be picked, because the calendar is disabled, the day is outside `min` or `max`, `isDateDisabled` rules it out, or a range cannot end on it. The grid still builds the weeks, and the first column still follows `firstDayOfWeek`.

`showOutsideDays` draws the leading and trailing days as buttons, in the same muted color as the weekday names, and marks them with `data-outside`. It is off by default. With it off, those cells stay empty, whether or not `renderDay` is set. Passing `renderDay` does not add those days and does not make them pressable. With `showOutsideDays`, `renderDay` is called for them too. Arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down still move the focused date, and the shown month follows that date.

The day button stays the one Tab stop. Its name is the full date, and when the day has content, the date and then that content, so a screen reader reads the date and then the content. The name holds every word the day shows, so a speech user can say what they see (WCAG 2.5.3, Label in Name). The day number is in the locale's digits, as the date in the name is. Put text in `renderDay`, such as an event title or a "+N more" count. Interactive content is not supported. Something focusable inside the day is invalid, and this slot gives it no keyboard path. In development, `Calendar.Grid` logs an error when `renderDay` returns one. A product puts the interaction on the day press itself. The day button's press, Enter and Space are how to act on that day.

## Accessible composition

**Todae handles:** `Calendar.Root` is a `role="group"`. `Calendar.Grid` is a `role="grid"` table, labelled by the header's month while a `Calendar.Header` is rendered, with a column header for each weekday whose `abbr` is the full name. It is one Tab stop: arrow keys move by day and week, Home and End to the ends of the week, Page Up and Page Down by month, and with Shift by year. Left and Right swap in a right-to-left page. Each day is a button named with the full date. When the day has content, the name is the date and then that content. The day number is hidden from the name, since the date already says it. The selected one's cell has `aria-selected`, and today has `aria-current="date"`. Dates `isDateDisabled` rules out have `aria-disabled`, and days outside `min` and `max` are disabled. The month and year is a button with `aria-expanded`, true while the years show, inside a live region, so a screen reader hears it change. The page of years is a `role="grid"` with the same single Tab stop: arrow keys move by year and row, Home and End to the ends of the row, and Page Up and Page Down by page. The buttons beside the heading are named from the `previousMonth` and `nextMonth` strings in [StringsProvider](/utilities/strings-provider), or `previousYears` and `nextYears` while the years show, and they and the heading draw the `chevron` glyph from [GlyphsProvider](/utilities/glyphs-provider). `disabled`, or a disabled [Fieldset](/fieldset), disables every button. In range mode, the grid and the page of years have `aria-multiselectable`, and every day or year the range touches has `aria-selected`, or while a range waits for its end, only the first pick's. After the first pick, the `pickEndDate` string is announced through a live region, and the days that can't end the range have `aria-disabled` and still take focus. Escape, or a modal's close request such as the Android back gesture, then drops the first pick and stops there, so a popover or dialog around the calendar stays open.

**You supply:** A name for the group, with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, and one for the grid when there is no `Calendar.Header`. `locale` when the page's language is not the browser's.

**Watch for:** Ruling dates out with `isDateDisabled` but not saying why. Explain the rule nearby, such as "Weekdays only".

## When to use

**When:** Pick one date from a calendar shown on the page, such as on a booking screen.

**When not:** A date in a form field → [Field.DateInput](/field/date-input), with this calendar in a [Field.Picker](/field/picker). A range of dates in a form field → [Field.DateRangeInput](/field/date-range-input). A date with a time → [Field.DateTimeInput](/field/date-time-input), or the [Date and time picker](/recipes/date-time-picker) recipe.
