# Field.ColorPicker

`Field.ColorPicker` is the field's control: a button inside a [Field](/field), drawn as a swatch one field line tall and filled with its color. It opens a [ColorPicker](/color-picker) in a [Popover](/popover), and a press elsewhere in the field opens it too. Its value is a `#rrggbb` string, and `onValueChange` gets each new color as it is dragged or keyed. To show the value as text, put a `Field.Affix` after it. On its own, the field is as wide as the swatch.

```tsx
<Label.Root htmlFor={id}>Accent</Label.Root>
<Field.Root id={id}>
	<Field.ColorPicker value={color} onValueChange={setColor} />
	<Field.Affix>{color}</Field.Affix>
</Field.Root>
```

The picker opens and closes through `open` with `onOpenChange`, or `defaultOpen`. `onOpenChange` gets the reason it closed: `'escape'`, `'outside'`, `'trigger'`, or `'disabled'` when the field becomes disabled. Open, the picker takes focus, and Escape closes it and returns focus to the swatch.

## Accessible composition

**Todae handles:** The button takes `id`, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby` from `Field.Root`, and native `disabled` and `aria-invalid` from the Root or a surrounding [Fieldset](/fieldset). It gets `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` while the picker is open. The popover is a `role="dialog"` named from the `colorPicker` string in [StringsProvider](/utilities/strings-provider), and its inputs are named and keyed as on [ColorPicker](/color-picker).

**You supply:** A name, through a [Label](/label) whose `htmlFor` is the Root `id` or `aria-label` / `aria-labelledby` on the Root.

**Watch for:** The swatch's name is the label, not the color, so a screen reader does not hear the value from the button. Put the value in a `Field.Affix` when people need to read it.

## When to use

### Field.ColorPicker

**When:** Pick one color in a form, such as a brand or accent color.

**When not:** A color in another form, such as `oklch()` → `Field.TextInput`, with a `Field.ColorPicker` in its own field beside it for picking. A picker shown inline, without a field → [ColorPicker](/color-picker). A fixed set of colors → RadioGroup.
