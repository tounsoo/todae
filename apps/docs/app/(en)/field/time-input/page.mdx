# Field.TimeInput

`Field.TimeInput` is a text input inside a [Field](/field) that people type a time of day into, in the locale's own way. Its `value` is a `Temporal.PlainTime` or `null`. It reads the text when it is committed, on blur, on Enter or when its form is submitted, rewrites it as `formatTime` writes the time and calls `onValueChange` with it. Typing alone calls nothing. Without `onValueChange`, the input is read-only. It works like [Field.DateInput](/field/date-input) and takes the same props where they mean the same thing.

<Example of={FieldTimeInputPicker} block />

Here a [Field.Picker](/field/picker) beside the input opens an [OptionList](/optionlist) of times in a [Popover](/popover), made with [timeOptions](/utilities/time-options). The input and the list share one time, so a typed time between two steps, such as `9:07`, is in the list in its place. A pick closes the popover. Beside a `Field.TimeInput`, the picker's button shows a clock glyph and is named from the `timePicker` string.

## Typed times {/*#typed-times*/}

`formatTime` writes a time in the locale's short time style, such as `9:30 PM` in `en-US` and `21:30` in `de`, and `parseTime` reads one typed with or without its minutes and the locale's word for the part of the day, such as `9:30 pm` or `2130`. [formatTime and parseTime](/utilities/format-time) says what each writes and reads.

`Field.TimeInput` reads its text with `parseTime` and writes it with `formatTime`, in its `locale`. Empty text commits `null`. Text that is not a time stays as it was typed, `onValueChange` is not called, and the field is marked invalid once a commit has tried to read it; until the text is a time or empty, the form can't be submitted.

## On its own

On its own, `Field.TimeInput` is the field's control, with no button. Any time can be typed, between the steps of a list too.

<Example of={FieldTimeInputAlone} block />

The time goes with a form in a native `<input type="time">` beside the text input, under the field's `name`, or the input's own `name`, the way HTML writes a time, such as `09:30`, or `09:30:15` with seconds. The text input sends nothing, and the native input is hidden from sight and from assistive technology and is not a tab stop. `required` makes a form with no time invalid, and `min` and `max` make a form with an earlier or later time invalid. A `min` later than `max` is an overnight range, as in a native time input: the form accepts the times from `min` to midnight and from midnight to `max`, and [timeOptions](/utilities/time-options) lists the same; a time in the gap between `max` and `min` is out of range, and the message names `min`, such as "Enter 10:00 PM or later". The browser checks all three on the native input, and text that is not a time blocks the submit too. Inside a [Form.Root](/form), a problem shows in the field's `ErrorMessage.Root` in the page's language, with `min` or `max` written as `formatTime` writes a time, and focus goes to the text input. Outside a `Form.Root` the browser blocks the submit and focus lands on the text input too.

## Temporal

Todae ships no Temporal polyfill. The input's value, `formatTime`, `parseTime` and `timeOptions` use `Temporal.PlainTime`, so where the browser has no Temporal, the app loads a polyfill (see [Native platform](/platform)). Todae's types need TypeScript 6 or later, which declares `Temporal`.

## Accessible composition

**Todae handles:** The text input takes `id`, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby` and `aria-describedby` from `Field.Root`, and native `disabled` and `aria-invalid` from the Root or a surrounding [Fieldset](/fieldset). It gets `aria-invalid` once a commit can't read its text, and `aria-required` with `required`. Beside a [Field.Picker](/field/picker), the input stays the field's control, and the picker's button is named from the `timePicker` string in [StringsProvider](/utilities/strings-provider) and shows the `clock` glyph from [GlyphsProvider](/utilities/glyphs-provider). The popover is a `role="dialog"` named from the same string. Open, focus goes to the list, where arrow keys, Home and End move through the times and Enter or Space picks one.

**You supply:** A name, through a [Label](/label) whose `htmlFor` is the Root `id` or `aria-label` / `aria-labelledby` on the Root. A name for the list of times, with `aria-label` on `OptionList.Root`. The same time for the input and the list, and the same `locale`, `min` and `max` for the input and `timeOptions`.

**Watch for:** With a `value`, the server writes the time in its own locale, so pass `locale` when the input renders on a server, and to `timeOptions` too. A list of times keyed by its labels: two locales write a time differently, so key options by their `value`.

## When to use

### Field.TimeInput

**When:** A time of day, such as opening hours, a daily reminder or when a meeting starts.

**When not:** A date and time in one value → [Field.DateTimeInput](/field/date-time-input). A duration → [Field.NumberInput](/field/number-input) with an `Affix` for the unit.
