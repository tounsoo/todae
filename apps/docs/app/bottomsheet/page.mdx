# BottomSheet

Modal custom portal. Not a native dialog. Scrim, focus trap, Escape, `aria-modal`, scroll lock. Mid and full snap. Drag from the grabber or from the body at `scrollTop === 0`. List scroll stays free.

<Example>
  <BottomSheetBasic />
</Example>

The portal re-applies appearance, density, and theme overrides. Extra classes on the provider node do not follow the portal.

## Consumer sketch

Touch Combobox later. A button opens this sheet. Search plus options live in the sheet. Desktop typeahead-while-open stays a popover in the Combobox PRD.
